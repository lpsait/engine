The numbers

10 min

Capacity and compression ratio explain half of what modifications do. Use the calculator to explore, then try the four challenges by hand.

Swept volume (one cylinder) = π/4 × bore² × stroke
Engine capacity = swept volume × number of cylinders
Compression ratio = (swept volume + clearance volume) ÷ clearance volume

Work in millimetres, then divide by 1,000 to turn mm³ into cc.
